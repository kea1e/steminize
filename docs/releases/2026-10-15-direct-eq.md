## Interaction

- Click or touch the graph to grab the nearest existing band on the logarithmic frequency axis. Focus is always available; enabled low/high cuts are selectable too. No new filter bands.
- Drag focus horizontally for frequency (40 to 12,000 Hz) and vertically for gain (-24 to +12 dB). Drag a cut horizontally within its existing slider limits (low cut 20 to 10,000 Hz; high cut 50 to 20,000 Hz). Width, other bands and musical defaults stay unchanged.
- Active handle and frequency/gain readout follow the gesture. Existing sliders, presets, keyboard editing, one-step mixer undo/redo and library persistence retain their original paths.
- Pointer capture plus gesture-scoped window listeners cover release outside the graph, including silent capture loss observed in the headless browser. Release, cancellation and lost capture finish once and remove listeners.
